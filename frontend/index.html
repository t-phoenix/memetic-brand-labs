Memetic Brand Labs

Communication intelligence for emerging technology companies.

Memetic Brand Labs is an adpr initiative that builds communication systems with memetic potential for AI, Web3, infrastructure and deep tech founders—helping products become easier to understand, easier to remember, and harder to ignore.

We help founders transform complex technical ideas into shared language, recognizable narratives, and repeatable cultural signals. The technology may be clear; the story often is not. That is why we started MBL.

Who is Memetic Brand Labs for?

MBL works with AI company founders, Web3 innovators, infrastructure teams, and deep tech builders whose products are strong but whose stories are hard to explain, remember, or share.

What does the Memetic Brand Workshop include?

A focused working session where founders leave with a Memetic Brand Direction, Memetic Brand Voice, Narrative & Content Playbook, and creative directions with memetic potential.
